`/compare` sends one question to two or three AIs at the same time. Answers stream side by side (stacked in narrow terminals) with the time to the first word and the total time; the fastest is marked.

## Usage

```text
/compare what is the difference between let and const?
/compare groq gemini openrouter explain CORS in one paragraph
/compare groq/llama-3.3-70b-versatile gemini/gemini-2.5-flash write a regex for emails
```

- Leading words that are provider IDs (or `provider/model`) choose the AIs. Up to 3 are used.
- Without them, The Atlas takes the first three connected AIs.
- You need at least two connected AIs. Connect more with `/connect`.

Type `/compare` with no question to arm it: the prompt changes to `⇄` and your next message is compared. `Esc` disarms it.

`@file` attachments work in compared questions too.

## Pick the answer

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| `←` `→` | Choose an answer |
| `Enter` | Keep it |
| `Esc` | Keep none; stops answers still streaming |

All answers are printed in the chat. The kept answer joins the conversation, and its AI becomes the current model, so you can continue from there.
